        <p id="tribute-info">NASA's primary Florida presence is the Kennedy Space Center (KSC) on Merritt Island, a major launch complex vital for human spaceflight, research, and exploration, featuring iconic structures like the massive Vehicle Assembly Building (VAB) where rockets like the Saturn V, Space Shuttle, and SLS are assembled, plus facilities for astronaut prep and spacecraft checkout, supporting both government and commercial missions with its equatorial location and Atlantic launch corridor.<br><br><span class="bold">Key Facilities & Functions:</span>
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Kennedy Space Center (KSC): NASA's launch hub, supporting science, technology, exploration, and human spaceflight, adjacent to Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.</li>
  <li>Vehicle Assembly Building (VAB): Assembles massive rockets (Saturn V, Shuttle, SLS) vertically, preparing them for launch; it's one of the world's largest buildings by volume.
</li>
<li>Operations & Checkout Building (O&C): Houses astronaut quarters, suit-up areas, and spacecraft workshops for processing crewed vehicles. 
</li>
</ul>
